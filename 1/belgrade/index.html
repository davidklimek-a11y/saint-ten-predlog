Belgrade, the way we'd show you

Out of the door and a few minutes on foot to the Temple of Saint Sava; the rest of the city is close.

Our short list

  • Temple of Saint Sava

    A few minutes' walk; one of the largest Orthodox temples in the world.

  • Vračar

    Our neighbourhood, best seen on foot with a coffee to go.

  • Kalenić market

    Where Belgrade's everyday life meets tradition.

  • Nikola Tesla Museum

    About five minutes' walk from the hotel.

  • Skadarlija

    Belgrade's bohemian street of kafanas.

  • Knez Mihailova & Republic Square

    The pedestrian heart, lined with buildings from the 1870s.

  • Kalemegdan

    Where Belgrade began, above the meeting of the Sava and the Danube.
