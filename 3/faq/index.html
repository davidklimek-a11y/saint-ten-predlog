Good to know

How far is the Temple of Saint Sava?

A few minutes on foot — about 380 metres.

How far is the airport?

About 20 km. We can arrange an airport pick-up; just ask.

Is there parking?

The hotel has a garage. Write to us about availability and the current price.

Can I bring my dog?

We love guests who travel with their dog. Write to us before you book so we can confirm the details.

What comes with every room?

Wi-Fi, international landline calls, an espresso and tea machine, fitness and sauna, the business centre and anti-allergy pillows.

When are the sauna and fitness open?

Every day, 6:00–22:00, for hotel guests.

How do I book a massage?

Massages run daily 9:00–20:00 on request. Write to info@saintten.com.

How do I book a table at L'Adresse?

Lunch 12–17 h, dinner 18–23 h. Call +381 11 4116633 or write to info@saintten.com.

Which cancellation terms apply?

It depends on the rate you choose: flexible, or non-refundable for 5% less. The booking page shows the terms before you pay.

In which currency do you charge?

Prices are in euros; cards are charged in dinars at the National Bank of Serbia rate.

Do you host meetings and private dinners?

Yes: a 110 m² meeting space and a 72 m² room with a terrace. Write to sales@saintten.com.

How do I buy a gift voucher?

Order a night or a massage voucher from sales@saintten.com or +381 11 411 66 33.
