1929

A house with a signature

Architect Stojan Veljković built the house at Svetog Save 10 in 1929. His family, long in Belgrade politics, also owned the first Bank of Vračar.

Source saintten.com, history

Symbols in plain sight

Veljković is said to have been a mason. Look up: the façade still carries the symbols.

Source saintten.com, history

1939

Where history held its meetings

The house hosted many political gatherings of former Yugoslavia; the one people remember is the Cvetković–Maček agreement.

Source saintten.com, history; agreement signed 26 August 1939 (public record)

The rumour we won't confirm
